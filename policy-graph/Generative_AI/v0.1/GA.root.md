# Generative AI Image Classification

## Decision rule
Classify an image as `gen_ai` when visible evidence, provenance evidence, or a combination of cues makes it more likely than not that the image is fully or materially generated by a generative model under the current policy version.

Classify as `not_gen_ai` when the evidence better supports an authentic photograph, conventional edit, CGI/game render, benign illustration, compression artifact, or when evidence is insufficient.

  - [[GA.visual_artifacts.anatomy.hands|anatomical hand/limb artifacts]]

## Cold-start stance
This v0.1 graph is intentionally conservative. It should create useful places to attach examples, justifications, and SME corrections; it is not final policy law from Mount Olympus.
