# Generative AI Image Classification

## Decision rule
Classify an image as `gen_ai` when visible evidence, provenance evidence, or a combination of cues makes it more likely than not that the image is fully or materially generated by a generative model under the current policy version.

Classify as `not_gen_ai` when the evidence better supports an authentic photograph, conventional edit, CGI/game render, benign illustration, compression artifact, or insufficient-evidence routing.

Plausible camera-like features such as shallow depth of field, golden-hour lighting, telephoto bokeh, high resolution, sensor-like noise, or coherent overall composition do **not** by themselves rule out `gen_ai`. They support `not_gen_ai` only after the reviewer has checked for specific positive cues.

## Required review order
1. Check explicit provenance or disclosure.
2. Check positive visual nodes, including anatomy, eyes, text/symbols, repeated details, surface texture, scene geometry/reflections, and nature/wildlife synthesis artifacts.
3. Only then assign authentic-photo, conventional-edit, CGI/game-render, compression, or low-quality boundary nodes.
