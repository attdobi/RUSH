RUSH = Reinforcement learning Using high SME-in-the-loop and High reasoning models.

RUSH combines independent high-reasoning model votes, Obsidian-style Markdown policy graphs, and SME-reviewed ground truth so disagreements become versioned decision-quality infrastructure instead of hidden uncertainty. The first pilot cold-starts GenAI image classification.

High-reasoning committee

Models vote independently, cite policy evidence, and expose disagreements for SME review instead of hiding uncertainty behind a single label.

Obsidian policy graph

Markdown nodes with frontmatter, examples, graph edges, and source anchors capture every category, boundary, exception, and clarification.

Consensus ≠ truth

SME labels are gold; consensus is a risk signal for routing, audits, and policy iteration — not ground truth.
