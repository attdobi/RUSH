SME-in-the-loop policy intelligence

Turn expert judgment into a living policy graph, then use LLMs to find the cases humans should re-check.

RUSH starts with SME-quality examples, builds a versioned policy graph, and treats high-reasoning LLMs as misalignment detectors — not ground truth. This demo cold-starts GenAI image classification and sets up bulk model labeling as the next step.

Consensus / majority vote

What did the panel decide — together?

For each image, every model casts a vote; this view shows how the panel agreed (or didn’t). Unanimous calls are green, plain majorities are blue, and ties are amber. Rows where the majority disagrees with the SME ground truth are highlighted for review.
