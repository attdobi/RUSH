Policy graph

Policy is the product, not a prompt blob.

Every label should point to a policy node: positive evidence, boundary negative, exception, or uncertainty. SMEs approve policy diffs; LLMs provide pressure tests and candidate ambiguities.

Decision quality

Assume SME quality in the demo. Use LLMs to surface what might be wrong.

For the VC story, the sampled labels are treated as SME-quality candidates. Bulk LLM labeling will compare models against that truth, then prioritize misalignments, hard negatives, and policy gaps for SME review.
